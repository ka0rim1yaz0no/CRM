# Combined CRM Release

The owner authorized GitHub publication under `ka0rim1yaz0no/CRM` and an online
deployment on October 21, 2026 (Asia/Manila), with employees paused. They chose
to preserve the live rollout switches and verify newer feature activation
separately. Publishing source is not equivalent to enabling every guarded feature.

## Included

- All accumulated source on `security-data-check` through the displayed-list
  auto-caller fix, plus the local startup preflight.
- The owner's cinematic login commit `7ff0d0059d0438ca67fcb9e5cd0189030a6d9d1c`,
  integrated without replacing authentication or business selection logic.
- Self-hosted opening video and GLB logo; no dependency on a local file URL.
- Video failure, autoplay rejection, missing media, visibility, disposal and
  loading deadline regressions.
- CI includes every frontend script test and limits Mongo-backed test concurrency
  to two workers. The queue tests now freeze the imported eligibility helpers'
  clock as well as the component clock; their prior result depended on the real
  Philippine time. Production call-window logic was not changed by that test fix.

No real call was placed. Actual physical Nextiva timing, every production business,
all mobile/device combinations and rollback recovery are not certified by these
local tests. The two optional external-Mongo skips remain unverified.

## Production Gates

Keep existing private configuration, uploads and employee/customer databases on
the VPS. Never deploy the local sample databases, backup archives or environment
files. Stage the complete source/build separately; retain the current live code,
process definitions and a current database/server recovery backup before switching.
Do not enable new security, ledger, payroll or automatic-timeout flags as a side
effect of publication. Verify production authentication prerequisites before
replacing the backend, and keep an immediate process/static-build rollback.

Production deployment status and evidence will be appended after the switch;
the checks above alone do not establish that online users have the new release.
